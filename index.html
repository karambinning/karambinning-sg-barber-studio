A studio, not a conveyor belt.

SG sits at the south end of Purfleet Street, a stone's throw from the Tuesday Market. It is small on purpose. Four chairs, one waiting bench, and a long mirror that catches the light from the front window most of the day.

Every cut starts with a conversation — what you want, what your hair will actually do, how long since the last one. Then we work, slowly. Skin fades are taken to the line. Beards are oiled and reshaped, not just trimmed. Scissor cuts are blow-finished if you ask.

No appointment? Walk in. There is usually a chair before lunch and a quieter slot late afternoon.
