We opened on Purfleet Street because it felt right — narrow shopfront, good window light, the kind of street people walk down on purpose.

SG is short for the founder, Sam. The studio took the name early and it stuck. Four chairs is the size the room wanted to be: enough that there's always someone working when you walk in, small enough that the cut you sit down for is the cut you get — no hand-offs, no apprentices learning on you, no rushing to clear the bench.

The work we do is straightforward. Skin fades taken to a clean line. Scissor cuts that hold their shape past the first wash. Beards trimmed and shaped with hot towels and oil, not just clipped flat. Junior cuts for boys who don't want to sit through forty-five minutes. Senior cuts at a price that hasn't moved in a while.

What gets us five-star reviews isn't a trade secret. It's the consultation. We ask what you want before we start, and we tell you what your hair will actually do if we try it. If you've sat in too many chairs that hand you a mirror and the wrong cut, that's the bit we get right.

Where to find us

Purfleet Street runs south from the Tuesday Market Place, through to the Custom House on the quay. We're at number 14, on the right as you walk down from the market. Most of King's Lynn is a ten-minute walk away.

How to book

Booksy is the simplest route — choose a barber, choose a slot, you're done. Walk-ins are welcome when there's a chair free; usually a quieter window before lunch and another late afternoon. If you're after a specific barber for a specific time, book it.
